Hands Up Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Hands Up ("the App") is published by Minseok Kim. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contacts. Everything you enter in the App (answers, logs, settings) is stored on your device only.

Camera and body position

Hands Up uses the front camera only while a round is open, after you tap Start a round. Each camera frame is read in memory on your device by Apple's Vision framework to find the position of your shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles, and is then discarded. The video is never shown, recorded, saved or uploaded, and no photos or images of you are kept.

What is kept on your device is only the result of each round: its length, how long your guard was up, how many times your hands dropped and the longest drop. These numbers are never uploaded. Deleting a session or the app removes them.

Voice cues

The coach's spoken cues ("Hands up", round calls) are generated on your device with Apple's speech synthesizer. The app does not use the microphone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework and does not send your purchase information to us or to any other company. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Analytics and advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell or share personal data. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the App deletes it. We hold no copy of it.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
