Alcohol Counter Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Alcohol Counter ("the App") is published by Minseok Kim. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contacts. Everything you enter in the App (answers, logs, settings) is stored on your device only.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework and does not send your purchase information to us or to any other company. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Analytics and advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell or share personal data. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the App deletes it. We hold no copy of it.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
