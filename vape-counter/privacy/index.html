Vape Counter – Nicotine Taper Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Vape Counter – Nicotine Taper ("the App") is published by Minseok Kim. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contacts. Everything you enter in the App (answers, logs, settings) is stored on your device only.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. To validate purchases and restore them on your devices, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated user identifier and your App Store receipt. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. We never see your payment details.

Analytics and advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell or share personal data. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the App deletes it. Anonymous purchase records held by RevenueCat can be deleted on request by emailing us.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
