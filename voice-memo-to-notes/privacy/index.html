Tidy Memo Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Tidy Memo ("the App") is published by Minseok Kim. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contacts. Everything you enter in the App (answers, logs, settings) is stored on your device only.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap record, the App uses your iPhone's microphone and Apple's speech recognition, set to run on your device only, to turn your words into text. The audio is processed in memory while you record. It is not saved, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. The App asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time you record, and you can turn either off at any time in Settings.

Notes

Your notes (title, summary, key points, to-dos and the transcript) are created on your iPhone and stored on your device only. On supported devices the App uses Apple's on-device language model to tidy the note; otherwise it uses its own on-device rules. Nothing is uploaded. When you tap Copy, the note is placed on your clipboard so you can paste it where you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework and does not send your purchase information to us or to any other company. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Analytics and advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell or share personal data. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the App deletes it. We hold no copy of it.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
